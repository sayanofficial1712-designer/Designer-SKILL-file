# Research to User Journey

Transform qualitative user research into a structured, evidence-based user journey using AI.

This workflow helps designers, researchers, and product teams analyse user research systematically and produce user journeys that are grounded in user evidence rather than assumptions.

Unlike generic prompting, this workflow follows a repeatable process that preserves user intent, surfaces pain points, captures emotions, and identifies actionable design opportunities.

---

## Why this exists

Creating a meaningful user journey is often one of the most time-consuming parts of UX synthesis.

Designers typically spend hours reading interview transcripts, clustering observations, identifying behavioural patterns, and manually converting those findings into journey maps.

While AI can dramatically accelerate this process, generic prompts often produce journeys that:

- Invent behaviours not supported by research
- Miss important emotional moments
- Ignore contradictory evidence
- Produce shallow or generic outputs
- Skip the reasoning behind design opportunities

This workflow addresses those problems by providing a structured methodology that guides AI through research synthesis before generating the final journey.

## Supported Inputs

The workflow accepts one or more of the following:

- User interview transcripts
- Observation notes
- Contextual inquiry findings
- Usability testing notes
- Workshop outputs
- Customer support conversations
- Research summaries
- Affinity mapping results

The research may come from one participant or multiple participants.

---

## Generated Outputs

The workflow produces a comprehensive user journey that includes:

- Actor
- Scenario
- User Goal
- Journey Phases
- User Actions
- Thoughts
- Emotions
- Pain Points
- Opportunities
- Supporting Evidence
- Design Recommendations

Every insight should be traceable back to research evidence.

---

## Workflow

The AI follows a structured synthesis process instead of generating a journey immediately.

```text
Research
      ↓
Extract observations
      ↓
Identify behavioural patterns
      ↓
Group into journey phases
      ↓
Map actions, thoughts & emotions
      ↓
Identify pain points
      ↓
Surface opportunities
      ↓
Generate final user journey
```

This ensures outputs remain explainable, consistent, and grounded in user research.

## Quick Start

1. Open `SKILL.md`
2. Provide your research data.
3. Use `template.md` as the expected output structure.
4. Refer to `example.md` to understand the desired quality and level of detail.
5. Review the generated journey and validate it against your original research.

## Design Principles

This workflow follows five principles:

- Evidence over assumptions
- Behaviour over opinions
- Consistency over creativity
- Explainability over speed
- Actionable outcomes over documentation
